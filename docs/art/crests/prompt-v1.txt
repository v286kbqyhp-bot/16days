Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one art-direction proposal contact sheet for a Chinese medieval-fantasy single-player tactical game; not a production sprite atlas.
Primary request: Design a coherent collection of exactly 17 different equipment crests, based on the existing gameplay roster below. These are wearable metal heraldic badges, not loose rune stones, potion bottles, cards or weapons.
Style: carefully drawn 2D PIXEL ART, visibly square pixels and stepped outlines, restrained 32x32-style icon complexity enlarged with nearest-neighbor appearance. No antialiasing on icons, no smooth painted rendering, no 3D renders, no excessive tiny ornamental noise. Each badge has a strong silhouette and one legible central motif. Warm aged metal fits a wooden medieval shop; dark enamel interiors; a very restrained accent color per crest.
Composition: beautiful clean landscape review board, 6 columns by 3 rows, first 17 cells populated in strict reading order; last cell is a small two-line legend, NOT another icon. Dark desaturated charcoal background, generous even spacing, consistent badge sizes. Heading exactly "纹章设计 · 第一版". Subtitle exactly "1 枚普通 · 16 枚传奇". Each icon has its short exact Chinese name beneath it, clear readable type. Do not include ability text, numbers, invented names, pricing or extra interface.
Shared visual grammar: ordinary badge has simple iron edging and a plain shield silhouette; legendary badges have a distinct warm brass double rim with a small diamond at the top. Family resemblance, but silhouettes and central motifs differ enough to identify without color alone. Highlights are sparse hard pixel clusters. Avoid external glow that weakens silhouettes.
